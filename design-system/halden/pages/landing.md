# Landing Page Overrides — Halden

> These rules override `MASTER.md` for the marketing landing page.
> Source: UX research (persona Richard, decision journey, competitor synthesis).

## Why these overrides exist

MASTER recommended sky-blue + orange CTA. Research says Richard is pitch-fatigued and arrives from HN/X already skeptical of "AI hype." Orange conversion energy reads as another cheap bot. Trust must be earned before price is argued.

## Color (research-led)

| Role | Hex | Why |
|------|-----|-----|
| Background | `#F6F3EC` | Cream paper from research docs — warm, clinic-like, not icy SaaS |
| Surface | `#FFFDF8` | Cards on cream |
| Text / ink | `#14130F` | Charcoal, 4.5:1+ on cream |
| Muted | `#5C584F` | Body secondary, still WCAG AA |
| Primary / CTA | `#1A4ED8` | Research blue `#1A56DB` — professional, not sales-orange |
| Dark section | `#12141A` | "When it breaks" product theatre |
| Live / human | `#C2410C` | Status only, never primary CTA |

Keep Lexend + Source Sans 3 from MASTER.

## Narrative order (Richard's decision journey)

1. **Arrive** — Hero: not another AI phone bot
2. **Trust** — What happens when it breaks + live human escalation with transcript
3. **Differentiate** — The mechanism that justifies 4×
4. **Value** — €890 framed against 31 recovered calls / week
5. **Act** — Low-friction CTA, no demo theatre

Do **not** lead with price. Price appears after trust + mechanism.

## Anti-patterns for this page

- AI hype, "revolutionary", "next-gen", glowing orbs
- Quote-only pricing
- Feature grids before the failure protocol
- Fake deep vertical fluency (research marks this as a Halden gap — stay honest)
